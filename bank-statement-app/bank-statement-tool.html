Bank Statement Generator

Build a D365 bank statement import file from a bank transactions CSV.

  1. Account
  2. Transactions
  3. Balances
  4. Generate

1 Which account are you reconciling?

Accounts come from the "List of Bank Accounts" tab of the mapping workbook. Accounts marked for exclusion or deletion are not listed.

2 Upload the transactions CSV

The bank export for this account. Required columns: Amount, Credit / Debit and Date. Also used where present: Account / IBAN, Currency, Transaction Narrative, Transaction Reference, Debtor Name, Creditor Name, Pending (P) / Historical (H).

3 Enter the opening and closing balances

Taken from the bank statement for this period. The opening balance plus the total of the uploaded transactions must equal the closing balance.

4 Generate the import file
